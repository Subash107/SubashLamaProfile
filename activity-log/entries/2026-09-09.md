## Research notes — the actual root cause

Subash downloaded his CV and no alert arrived, which was the right test to run: my
frontend fix was necessary but not sufficient.

**Bisected from the outside in.** No repository_dispatch had reached GitHub at all,
so the break was upstream of Actions. POSTed a synthetic resume-download to the
Worker: got 403. That was my error, not a bug — worker.js:19 keeps an ALLOWED_ORIGINS
list and a bare curl sends no Origin. Retried with
`-H "Origin: https://subashlamaprofile.pages.dev"` and got **502 "GitHub error"**,
which localised the fault precisely: the Worker is reached, enriches fine, and GitHub
refuses the dispatch.

First hypothesis was an expired GITHUB_PAT. Checked GITHUB_REPO first
(`Subash107/SubashLamaProfile`, correct — he has an old-origin remote pointing at a
now-deleted repo, so this was worth ruling out). Then dispatched with his own gh CLI
credential to isolate token from pipeline, and GitHub answered:

    422 — No more than 10 properties are allowed; 13 were supplied.

**That is the whole thing.** `repository_dispatch` caps client_payload at 10
properties. site.js sent 9 flat fields; worker.js:951-956 adds 6 more (ip, location,
org, lead_score, anon_flag, repeat). 15 total, rejected every time since the June 24
Worker change added lead_score/anon_flag/repeat. The Worker returns 502 to the
browser, the browser ignores it, and nothing anywhere records a failure. Completely
silent for 11 weeks.

Not the PAT at all. Worth remembering how close I came to blaming the credential —
the isolation dispatch is what settled it, and it cost one request.

**Fix without touching the Worker.** Only telegram-bot.js has a deploy workflow;
worker.js needs a manual `wrangler deploy`, which I cannot do. So the fix had to live
in site.js + the workflow: pack the page's 9 fields into 3 (`client` =
ts|os|browser|device|tz|lang, `referrer`, `utm` = source|medium) for a total of 9,
and unpack with `IFS='|' read`. The Worker's 6 are immovable, so the page's budget is
4 properties forever — noted in memory.

**Also closed a script-injection hole while in the file.** Every step interpolated
`${{ github.event.client_payload.* }}` directly into `run:` blocks. Those values are
caller-supplied, and the Origin allowlist is only enforced by browsers — I set the
header by hand from curl minutes earlier. A crafted referrer would have executed in
his runner with contents:write. Moved the whole payload into `env:` and switched the
steps to shell variables. Renamed the unpacked tz/lang to TZ_NAME/LANG_TAG because
TZ and LANG are meaningful to the shell.

**Verified end to end, not assumed.** 13 properties → 422. 10 → 204, workflow ran,
Telegram delivered (message_id 1209). After deploying the packed payload, a
browser-shaped POST through the Worker returned 200 (was 502), the workflow unpacked
`os=Windows 11/10 browser=Chrome device=Desktop utm=diagnostic`, and Telegram
delivered message_id 1211. Removed both diagnostic rows from resume-downloads.txt
afterwards — 18 lines back to the 16 genuine ones.
